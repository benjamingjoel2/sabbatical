Booking terms

The terms for a long stay planned with us, from first inquiry to coming home.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

These terms apply to journeys booked with Sabbatical, Second Home Lisboa, 1st Floor, Mercado da Ribeira, Avenida 24 de Julho, 1200-479 Lisboa, Portugal (email sabbatical@tourground.com). “You” means the lead traveller and everyone named on the booking. Please read them before you book.

How a booking is made

You tell us what you would like through our enquiry form or by email. We send you a proposed itinerary and a written quote. When you accept the quote and pay the deposit, we confirm your booking in writing; the contract between us starts on the date of that written confirmation. The lead traveller confirms they are over 18 and accepts these terms on behalf of everyone on the booking.

Prices

Prices on this website are shown “from” and are a per-person guide, usually based on two people sharing. Your price depends on dates, group size, accommodation and availability, and is set in your written quote. Once your booking is confirmed we will not increase the price, except for changes in government taxes, park or entry fees and fuel surcharges that are outside our control; we will tell you as soon as we know, and if the increase is more than 8% of your trip price you may cancel with a full refund.

Deposit and balance

A deposit, shown on your quote (usually 30% of the trip price), is due to confirm your booking. The balance is due 60 days before departure. If you book within 60 days of departure, the full price is due at booking. If the balance is not paid on time, we may treat the booking as cancelled by you and apply the cancellation charges below. Payment methods are set out in your quote.

What is included

Your quote lists exactly what is included, typically accommodation, the transport, activities and entrance fees described in your itinerary, and support from our team before and during your trip. Unless your quote says otherwise, it does not include international flights, visas, travel insurance, vaccinations and medical costs, tips, drinks, meals not listed, or personal expenses.

Changes by you

If you want to change your booking after confirmation, email us. We will try to help, but it depends on availability. You pay any extra cost charged by our suppliers. A change of travel dates or a large change to the itinerary may be treated as a cancellation and a new booking. You may transfer your place to another person who meets the trip’s conditions if you tell us at least 14 days before departure and pay any supplier charges for the change.

Cancellation by you

You may cancel at any time by email; cancellation takes effect on the day we receive it. Because we commit money to suppliers on your behalf, these charges apply, as a percentage of the total trip price:

  • 60 days or more before departure: the deposit;
  • 59 to 30 days: 50%;
  • 29 to 15 days: 75%;
  • 14 days or less, or if you do not travel: 100%.

Supplier costs that your quote marks as non-refundable (for example internal flights, permits or certain lodges) are charged in full whenever you cancel. Your travel insurance may cover these charges.

Changes or cancellation by us

Trips sometimes need small changes, such as a different vehicle or a similar room; we will tell you as soon as possible. If we must make a significant change before departure, you may accept it, take a comparable alternative, or cancel and receive a full refund of what you have paid. If we cancel your trip, we refund everything you have paid us. We will not cancel after the balance is paid except for reasons beyond our control (see “Events beyond our control”) or the safety of travellers.

Travel insurance

Comprehensive travel insurance is a condition of booking. It must cover cancellation and curtailment, medical treatment, emergency evacuation and repatriation, and the activities on your itinerary. Please send us the policy details before you travel.

Passports, visas and health

You are responsible for having a valid passport (normally valid for at least six months after your return, with blank pages), any visas and permits, and the vaccinations and health precautions required for your trip. Please check official government sources and a travel health professional before you go. We cannot be held responsible if you are refused travel or entry because of missing documents.

Our responsibility

We take reasonable care in planning your journey and choosing our suppliers, and we are responsible for providing the services we have confirmed in writing. We are not responsible for problems caused by you or someone on your booking, by a third party not connected with your trip that we could not have foreseen or avoided, or by events beyond our control. Except for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or where the law does not allow it, our liability is limited to the price of your trip. Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights.

Behaviour

Please respect local laws, customs, communities and wildlife, and follow the instructions of drivers, rangers and hosts on safety. If someone’s behaviour puts others at risk or seriously disrupts the trip, we may end their arrangements without a refund.

Complaints

If something is wrong during your trip, tell our team straight away so we can put it right. If it cannot be resolved on the spot, write to us at sabbatical@tourground.com within 28 days of your return and we will reply within 28 days.

Events beyond our control

We are not liable for failure or delay caused by events we could not foresee or avoid, such as war, civil unrest, terrorism, natural disasters, epidemics, severe weather, fire, government action, airport or port closures, or industrial action. If such an event stops your trip from going ahead, we will offer you an alternative or refund the money you have paid us, less any supplier costs we cannot recover, as the law allows.

Data protection

We use your personal data to arrange and run your trip as described in our Privacy Policy.

Governing law

These terms and any contract between us are governed by the laws of Portugal, and the courts of Lisbon have jurisdiction. If you live in the UK or the EU, you also keep the protection of the mandatory consumer laws of your home country and may bring proceedings there.
