Privacy policy

What we collect when you inquire, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

Sabbatical plans tailor-made trips abroad: one- to six-month stays in a furnished home with a local host. Our address is Second Home Lisboa, Mercado da Ribeira, Avenida 24 de Julho 49, 1200-479 Lisboa, Portugal. We decide how the personal data described here is used and are responsible for it. Write to us about anything on this page at sabbatical@tourground.com.

What we collect

  • Your enquiry: what you send through our enquiry form or by email: names, email address, phone number if you give one, travel dates, group size and ages of children, interests, budget band and your message.
  • Planning and booking details: once you ask us to plan or book a trip, what is needed to arrange it, such as names as they appear in passports, dates of birth, arrival and departure details, dietary needs and, only if you choose to tell us, health or access needs.
  • Newsletter: if a newsletter sign-up is offered and you use it, your email address.
  • Site usage: this site runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

We do not take payment card details through this site.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal, because you asked us to (steps taken at your request before a contract).
  • To plan, book and run your trip (performance of our contract with you).
  • To handle health or access needs you tell us about, with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep the accounting and booking records the law requires (legal obligation).
  • To look after our business, for example to deal with a complaint or a claim (our legitimate interests).

We only send you news or offers if you have asked for them, and you can stop them at any time. We never sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only those who need it: your host, the landlords of your home, and the coworking, insurance, language-school and vehicle providers you choose as add-ons, and the companies that provide our email, form handling and website hosting. Each receives only what it needs. We may also share data where the law requires it.

Your trip takes place outside the country where you may live, so the people delivering it will receive the details they need there. Where the law requires it, we put appropriate safeguards in place for those transfers, such as contractual commitments, or rely on the transfer being necessary to arrange the trip you asked for. You can ask us about them.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within two years of our last contact. Booking records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and then deleted. Health and access details are deleted once your trip is over, unless you ask us to keep them for a future trip.

Keeping it safe

We keep personal data in secure, access-controlled systems and share it only as described here. If something goes wrong that affects your data, we will tell you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or send it to you or someone else in a portable form. You can object to our using it on the basis of our legitimate interests, and withdraw any consent you have given. Write to sabbatical@tourground.com; we reply within one month. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Changes

We update this policy when our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
