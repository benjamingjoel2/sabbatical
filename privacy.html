Privacy policy

What we collect when you inquire, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

Sabbatical (“we”, “us”) runs sabbatical.tourground.com and plans private journeys. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Business address: Second Home Lisboa, 1st Floor, Mercado da Ribeira, Avenida 24 de Julho, 1200-479 Lisboa, Portugal.
Email: sabbatical@tourground.com

What we collect

  • Enquiry and booking forms: your name, email address, preferred start date, number of months and travellers, the add-ons you pick and your message, and anything else you choose to write in your message.
  • Booking details, once you go ahead: the names of everyone travelling, the passport details and dates of birth our suppliers need, arrival and departure information, and any dietary, medical or accessibility needs you tell us about (we use health details only with your explicit consent and only to arrange your trip safely).
  • Emails you send us and our replies.
  • Technical data: when you visit, our hosting provider records your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time, to keep the site secure and working.
  • Preferences stored in your browser, such as your language. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not build profiles of you and we never sell your data.

Why we use it and our legal bases

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal: steps taken at your request before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To book and run your trip, including passing details to the suppliers who provide it: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)); for health information, your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)).
  • To keep accounting and tax records: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To keep the website secure, prevent abuse and handle complaints or legal claims: our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Optional preferences stored on your device: your consent where it is required (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before.

Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs:

  • Vercel Inc. (United States) hosts this website. It processes the technical data in your requests (such as your IP address) and short-lived server logs.
  • Our email provider stores the messages you send to us and our replies.
  • Travel suppliers (hotels, lodges, transport, activity operators and local hosts) receive the details needed to provide your booked services.
  • Professional advisers, insurers and public authorities, where the law requires it or to establish or defend legal claims.

Our service providers act on our instructions under data processing agreements.

International transfers

Some providers, including Vercel, are based in the United States, and your trip suppliers are located in the country you travel to. Where data leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on adequacy decisions (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Transfers to suppliers delivering your trip are necessary to perform our contract with you (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)).

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking: 24 months after our last contact.
  • Booking and payment records: 7 years after your trip, for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Health and dietary details: deleted within 3 months after your return.
  • Server logs: a few days to 30 days, depending on our hosting provider.
  • Preferences in your browser: until you clear them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • erase your data;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • give you your data in a portable format;
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests (objection);
  • withdraw consent at any time.

Email sabbatical@tourground.com; we reply within one month. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular where you live or work, or where you think the problem occurred.

Cookies

This site uses only what it needs to work and to remember your choices. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

Children

Our website is not aimed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data online. Details of children travelling on a booking are given to us by a parent or guardian and used only to arrange the trip.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit access to the people who need it, and choose providers with strong security practices.
