Cookie policy

What this site stores in your browser, listed exactly.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Websites can also keep information in your browser’s local storage or session storage, which works in a similar way. In this policy, “cookies” covers all of these.

What this site uses

Sabbatical sets no cookies. We use only the items below, which are needed for the site to work or to remember choices you make. None of them is used for advertising or to track you across other websites.

NamePurposeDurationType
sabbatical.savedRemembers the packages you save so you can compare them later.Until you clear itLocal storage, preference
sabbatical.inquiriesKeeps a copy of an inquiry you send, with its reference code, on your own device.Until you clear itLocal storage, essential

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Because these items are strictly necessary or only store choices you make yourself, they do not need consent under the ePrivacy rules; we do not set any other cookies.

Third-party content

  • Fonts are served from our own website, so no font provider receives your data.
  • Photographs and video are served from our own website.

Our hosting provider, Vercel, records technical data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the site securely; it sets no cookies on this site.

How to manage cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings (usually under Privacy or Site data). Clearing this site’s data removes the items listed above; the site will still work, but it will forget your choices. Your browser’s help pages explain how: look for “clear cookies and site data” in Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.

Changes

If we start using new cookies, we will update this page and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.
