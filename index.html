How it works

Three steps, then you pack.

  1. Pick a place and a length

    Every package has a fixed home, a host and a set of inclusions. Choose one to six months and see the price change as you go.

  2. Send an inquiry

    Tell us when you'd like to arrive and who's coming. A host replies within two working days to confirm dates and answer questions.

  3. Land and settle in

    Airport pickup, keys, a stocked kitchen and a walk around the neighbourhood in your first week. The rest of the stay is yours.
